Naming convention

Every file on the portal follows the same pattern. Name your files the same way before uploading them.

PEI_Campaign_Language_Size_Type_Date_vN.ext

  • Fields are separated by "_"; words inside a field are joined with "-". No spaces or accents.
  • Always the same order. A field that does not apply is left out.
FieldValues
PEIAlways first (Poulin Électrique Inc).
CampaignIO1-Generac, IO2-Poulin, FB-Generac-Service (Facebook ad), Evanov (whole portal).
LanguageFR, EN or BI (bilingual).
SizeWidth x height in pixels: 300x250, 1920x1080.
TypeLeft out for a creative. FINAL (final upload), Proof-Static, Proof-Video, Poster, CreativeList, Display (ZIP), Logo-Horse.
DateYYYY-MM-DD (or YYYY-MM).
Versionv2, v3… The first version has no number.
